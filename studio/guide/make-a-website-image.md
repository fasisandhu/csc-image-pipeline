# Make a website image

Everything happens on the **Create image** tab, in six steps. Nothing is published or sent anywhere.

## 1. Type your idea, or skip and pick

Describe the image in your own words, for example "olive oversized hoodie, plain, product photo", and press **Fill from my words**. What Studio picked up shows as ticks under the box. You can also skip the box and pick everything yourself.

## 2. Answer what is left

Choices already made fold away to one line; press **Change** to edit one. The questions still to answer stay open. A greyed option can't be used with your other choices, and the line under it says why. Press **?** next to a question for a short tip.

## 3. Check "Your image" and the cost

The **Your image** card lists every choice and the estimated cost, plus the most it could cost if every allowed fix runs. Open **Show the full prompt** to read exactly what the pipeline wrote for the image model.

## 4. Optional: see {{sketches.count}} free sketches

**See {{sketches.count}} free sketches** makes quick, rough layouts at no cost. Click the one you like, and **Approve & create** builds on its layout. Sketches are only for trying ideas; they never go on the website.

## 5. Approve & create

This makes the real image. Anything estimated over ${{budget.ask_over_usd}} asks you to confirm first, and spending never passes ${{budget.cap_usd}} a month.

If your colour has no approved reference packshot yet, Studio asks you to make that packshot first. Then come back and create your shot.

## 6. See your image

The finished image appears at the top of the **Your image** card, with its caption. Every AI image carries one (see Start here).

Checks and approval come in the Review tab (next step).
